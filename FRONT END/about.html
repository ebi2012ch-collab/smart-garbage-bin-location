Project Overview

What Is This System?

SmartBin is an official waste management service portal developed for Adama City Administration. It allows residents to find active garbage bin locations, check fill statuses, and report waste management issues directly to local sanitation teams.

Built to support Adama City's vision of becoming a smart, clean, and sustainable urban environment, this platform bridges the gap between citizens and waste collection services.
